How PulseCoach works

"Today's readout" vs. "This week"

The four rings at the top only count entries logged today — they reset to 0 every midnight. The chart below them shows the last 7 days, so older entries stay visible there even after today's rings have reset. Two different time windows, both correct at the same time.

Logging your data

Use the "Log manually" form to add an entry by hand, or just tell your Coach what you did in plain language — "I drank 300ml of water" — and it'll log it for you automatically. Vague messages like "I drank some water" won't log anything; the coach will ask you for a number instead of guessing.

Today's entries list

Every entry you've logged today — manual or via chat — shows up in the list under the manual entry form. Made a mistake? Click the ✕ next to any entry to delete it; the rings and chart update immediately.

Demo data

"Load demo data" fills in a realistic 7-day history so you can see the app without manually logging a week's worth of numbers. It's always the same numbers by default; hit "🔀 Shuffle" if you want fresh random data instead. Your own manual entries are never touched by this.

Email notifications

You'll get a welcome email on signup, a security alert on every login, and a reset link if you use "Forgot password?". None of these are marketing emails.

Your daily goals

Set targets that fit you — the rings on your dashboard measure progress against these.
